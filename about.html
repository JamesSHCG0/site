About:

The Film

Project Hail Mary is based on the novel by Andy Weir, author of The Martian. It follows Ryland Grace, a science teacher who wakes up alone on a spaceship with no memory of how he got there. As he works out his mission, he finds he may not be so alone after all.

Ryan Gosling

Ryan Gosling stars as Ryland Grace within the Project Hail Mary film. He is known for films such as Drive, La La Land, Blade Runner 2049 and Barbie.
